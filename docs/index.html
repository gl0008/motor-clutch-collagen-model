Gated negative result

V4 · new-contact plasticity

Only fibre pairs that truly approach under load may form stress-free weak links. The baseline forms one and shows no resolved excess residual.

  • elastic vs new-link load–unload
  • no old-link rest reset
  • no remodeling claim
Open corrected V4
Why V4 is not made artificially dramatic: the accepted 5 nN elastic baseline does not bring enough new fibre pairs together to produce measurable plasticity. This is a useful model result: the next hypothesis must change loading history or plastic mechanism explicitly, not silently tune animation geometry.
