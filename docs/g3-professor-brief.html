The 30-second answer

G3 is not an attempt to make migration merely look more realistic. It removes the prescribed 0.65 polarity from G2 and replaces it with a testable causal chain. G3A lets a protrusion grow to collagen, capture a material point that moves with the fibre, and load a crosslinked 99-fibre network through motor–clutch forces. G3B lets polarity emerge from conserved intracellular activity; collagen can feed back only after attachment and load transmission. G3C then releases cell x/y translation and rotation through equal-and-opposite clutch reactions. The fresh rerun verifies that attachment, force transfer and reaction bookkeeping execute, but it does not yet show that aligned collagen stabilizes polarity and it is not realistic tumor migration.

How to read the three animations

G3A

Can one protrusion grab and pull?

The cell is intentionally fixed and one diagnostic protrusion is prescribed. Watch the green shaft grow, the tip encounter collagen, red clutch spokes appear, and the contacted fibre plus its crosslinked neighbors respond.

Say: this isolates attachment and force transmission. Do not call it polarity or migration.

G3B

Can the cell make its own front?

The cell remains fixed. Membrane color is the cell-intrinsic activity pool; green shafts are expressed protrusions. Feedback OFF/ON use the same network and stochastic seed. Four-second persistence and hysteresis stop the active protrusion from teleporting between nearly tied sectors.

Say: symmetry breaking executes without 0.65. The current pair does not show collagen stabilization.

G3C

Can clutch reactions move the cell?

Fixed and released cells share the same network. Only the released cell integrates the opposite clutch force and torque. The trajectory is true scale; the tiny motion is not visually amplified.

Say: reaction-driven motion is nonzero and hidden-drive controls are zero. Do not call the speed predictive.

Likely professor questions

Where does polarizability come from?

From redistribution and competition of a conserved cell-intrinsic activity pool, not a fixed probability. Noise breaks symmetry; self-reinforcement and local diffusion shape the patch. Collagen enters only through successful loaded adhesion.

Why does the protrusion no longer jump?

An incumbent is retained for at least 4 s. A challenger must exceed it by both a 1.25 ratio and a 0.03 absolute activity margin. Geometry is continuous: the old protrusion retracts while the new one grows.

Does fibre “mass” matter?

This is an overdamped model, so inertial mass is intentionally absent. Size matters through cell radius, fibre diameter/segment length, elastic stiffness and effective drag. The unresolved comparison is therefore size–stiffness–drag, not a Newtonian cell/fibre mass ratio.

Why 99 fibres?

It prevents the mechanism from depending on one hand-picked fibre and preserves a boundary-connected crosslinked network. It is still a synthetic 2D sample, not a measured collagen density distribution.

Why is the deformation so small?

The current motor scale and stiff elastic network produce sub-resolution displacement. The motor-off difference shows it is not numerical drift, but its magnitude signals that force, fibre stiffness, drag and density need matched calibration.

What is the strongest negative result?

G3B feedback ON and OFF are indistinguishable in the fresh pair, and the four-seed aligned ensemble is not better than isotropic. The architecture is working; the intended guidance effect is not yet established.

Recommended revision priority after tomorrow

  1. Validate G3B before tuning G3C. Preregister a larger aligned/isotropic/rotated/feedback-off ensemble and outcome metrics; do not tune against held-out seeds.
  2. Calibrate the upstream observables. Fit protrusion lifetime, attachment probability, clutch force/traction and collagen displacement before asking the model to reproduce migration speed.
  3. Run sensitivity and convergence. Capture range, contact stiffness, activity gain/noise/timescale, bead spacing, Gaussian width, fibre density and crosslink density.
  4. Replace synthetic geometry with measured statistics. Use collagen orientation, pore size, fibre-length and density distributions from the target experimental system.
  5. Only then calibrate cell motion. Jointly fit whole-cell drag and active force to traction plus speed; after that consider deformable cell/nucleus, 3D pores, transient crosslinks, plasticity and proteolysis.

A two-minute presentation script

We begin with the limitation of G2: it demonstrates force transmission through crosslinked collagen, but its moving-cell direction still uses a human-selected polarization probability. G3 divides migration into three independently falsifiable stages. First, G3A fixes the cell and asks whether a protrusion can grow at finite speed, capture a material point after its tip reaches a continuous collagen segment, generate traction through tension-only clutches, a motor force–velocity law and Bell rupture, and transfer that load through bead–spring and crosslink mechanics to 99 fibres. The fresh control gives 0.001171 µm maximum motor-on bead displacement while the motor-off response is effectively zero, so the force path exists. Second, G3B removes 0.65 and uses conserved intracellular activity on 24 sectors. It can break symmetry internally; collagen feeds back through the adhesion score only after a clutch binds and carries load. Minimum lifetime and switching hysteresis prevent protrusions from teleporting. However, the fresh ON/OFF pair is identical and the aligned ensemble does not outperform the isotropic ensemble, so collagen guidance cannot yet be claimed. Third, G3C gives the cell an equal-and-opposite reaction and torque for every collagen force, with no added migration velocity. The released cell moves 0.003122 µm while fixed and empty controls remain zero. This verifies reaction bookkeeping, but upstream guidance and drag remain uncalibrated. The current conclusion is therefore a physics-based mechanism demonstration, not realistic tumor migration. The next priority is a larger preregistered G3B ensemble and calibration against protrusion, traction and collagen-displacement data before tuning migration speed or adding 3D complexity.
