What is evidence, and what is an assumption?

Direct mechanism support

Aligned collagen can guide MDA-MB-231 migration

Riching et al. found more persistent motion and constrained protrusions along aligned collagen. Carey et al. observed radially aligned fibers stabilize and enlarge nearby probing protrusions.

Indirect support

A successful protrusion can define a front

Aligned matrices reduce protrusion number and prolong the dominant protrusion, but the exact 120 s memory clock is not measured by these papers.

Model assumption

Natural rear release, then no rear rebinding

Rear clutches are never forced to break. They follow the same Bell law; only after a complete natural site failure is its next search restricted to the forward half-plane.

Provisional parameters

Probability gains and cone widths

\(\beta_a=\beta_m=2\), 45° front cone, 15° tract SD, and 120 s memory are declared sensitivity variables—not literature-fitted values.

    However — what v4E still cannot claim

    v4E tests whether two plausible single-cell mechanisms are sufficient in this 2D elastic network. It does not model a deformable cell, organoid surface-only traction, leader cells, crosslink rupture, plastic remodeling, proteolysis, or 3D steric confinement. A positive result is a mechanism demonstration; a negative E1/E2/E3 result is retained rather than hidden or repaired with larger forces.
