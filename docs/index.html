Overall G1 flaw → the specification for G2

  • Replace all-end anchoring with outer-boundary-only anchoring and a connectivity gate.
  • Use a 180 µm, 99-fibre, physically scaled nN–µm network with compression softening.
  • Show every bead, spring, crosslink, contact, force direction and anchor.
  • Calibrate cell speed and make fixed/moving conditions visibly separable without exaggeration.
  • Require plastic residual to exceed an elastic load–unload control.
