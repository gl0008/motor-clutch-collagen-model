cumulative design

Each block has a stopping gate

v4-A · mechanics controls

Passive fixed-size cell must do nothing. A prescribed contractile cavity separately tests the bending-to-stretching recruitment mechanism.

However: cavity contraction is a boundary condition, not biological traction and not cell shrinkage.

v4-B · fixed cell

Normal traction acts only on surface-band material points. A dipole is a same-total-force control. \(F_\parallel\) and \(F_\perp\) are measured predictions.

However: deterministic force cannot show attachment loading, failure or recoil.

v4-C · motor–clutch

Relative actin–ECM motion loads clutches after contact. Independent and equal-load-sharing sites are shown side by side.

However: a fixed cell records reaction but cannot translate.

v4-D · moving cell

Only translation is released. New site direction comes from the encountered local collagen tangent, never a global polarization value.

Boundary: this remains 2D, elastic and single-cell; no growth, plasticity, degradation or deformable nucleus.

all implemented equations

What every block computes

Key · overdamped bead force balance

\[\zeta_b\dot{\mathbf r}_i=\mathbf F_i^{\rm stretch}+\mathbf F_i^{\rm bend}+\mathbf F_i^{\rm crosslink}+\mathbf F_i^{\rm contact}+\mathbf F_i^{\rm steric}.\]

Drag converts the instantaneous net force into velocity. It dissipates motion but stores no viscoelastic memory.

Axial bead bond

\[\mathbf F_{ij}^{s}=k_{ij}(\ell_{ij}-\ell_{ij}^{0})\hat{\mathbf e}_{ij},\qquad k_{ij}=\frac{EA}{\ell_{ij}^{0}}.\]

The reported axial strain is \((\ell-\ell_0)/\ell_0\). Compression uses the inherited microbuckling softening.

Discrete bending

\[E_b=\frac{\kappa_b}{2}\sum_j\left\|\mathbf r_{j-1}-2\mathbf r_j+\mathbf r_{j+1}-\mathbf c_j^0\right\|^2,\qquad \mathbf F_i^{b}=-\frac{\partial E_b}{\partial\mathbf r_i}.\]

Bending change is the magnitude of the discrete curvature vector relative to its initial value.

Permanent elastic crosslink

\[E_x=\frac{k_x}{2}\left\|\big(\mathbf p_b-\mathbf p_a\big)-\boldsymbol\ell_x^0\right\|^2,\qquad \mathbf F_i^x=-\frac{\partial E_x}{\partial\mathbf r_i}.\]

Each endpoint is a continuous point on a fiber segment. Crosslinks transmit force but never break or reset in this revision.

Key control · contractile cavity

\[\mathbf r_i(t)=\big[1-\varepsilon(t)\big]\mathbf r_i(0),\qquad \varepsilon(t)=\varepsilon_{\max}\min\!\left(1,\frac{t}{t_{\rm ramp}}\right).\]

This prescribed displacement validates ECM recruitment only. It is never called a tumor cell.

Key contact rule · hard band then Gaussian

\[0\le d_s\le d_c,\qquad w_s=\frac{\exp(-d_s^2/\sigma_c^2)}{\sum_q\exp(-d_q^2/\sigma_c^2)},\qquad \mathbf F_s=w_sF_{\rm total}\hat{\mathbf n}_{\rm in}.\]

Noncontact fibers receive no Gaussian force. They can respond only through fiber bonds, steric contact or a crosslink path.

Key analysis · same force, two components

\[\mathbf F_{\parallel,s}=(\mathbf F_s\!\cdot\!\mathbf t_s)\mathbf t_s,\qquad \mathbf F_{\perp,s}=\mathbf F_s-\mathbf F_{\parallel,s}.\]

The model tests whether transverse loading associates with bending/rotation and axial loading with tension/transmission; it does not hard-code those outcomes.

Radial versus boundary orientation

\[\alpha_r=\cos^{-1}|\mathbf t\!\cdot\!\mathbf e_r|,\qquad \alpha_b=\cos^{-1}|\mathbf t\!\cdot\!\mathbf t_{\rm boundary}|.\]

Radial collagen has \(\alpha_r\approx0^\circ\), \(\alpha_b\approx90^\circ\). Boundary-parallel collagen has the reverse.

Shell-wise radial order

\[S_r=\left\langle2(\mathbf t\!\cdot\!\mathbf e_r)^2-1\right\rangle,\qquad \Delta S_r=S_r(t)-S_r(0).\]

\(S_r=1\) is radial, \(-1\) circumferential and approximately 0 isotropic. \(\Delta S_r\) removes finite-sample starting bias.

Key · continued clutch loading

\[\dot x_s=v_0\max\!\left(0,1-\frac{F_s}{F_{\rm stall}}\right)-(\mathbf v_{\rm ECM,s}-\mathbf v_c)\!\cdot\!\hat{\boldsymbol\ell}_s,\qquad F_c=k_cx_c.\]

Force keeps rising after contact because actin moves relative to ECM. It stops through ECM tracking, motor stall or rupture—not cell shrinkage.

Bell slip-bond rupture and rebinding

\[k_{\rm off}(f)=k_{\rm off}^0\exp\!\left(\frac{|f|}{F_b}\right),\qquad P_{\rm off}=1-e^{-k_{\rm off}\Delta t},\qquad P_{\rm on}=1-e^{-k_{\rm on}\Delta t}.\]

These are effective slip bonds. Catch bonding, reinforcement and focal-adhesion maturation are absent.

Key comparison · equal load sharing

\[f_i=\frac{F_{\rm site}}{i},\qquad r_i=i k_{\rm off}^0\exp\!\left(\frac{F_{\rm site}}{iF_b}\right),\qquad g_i=(N-i)k_{\rm on}.\]

As \(i\) falls, the remaining clutches carry more force and can fail cooperatively. The independent model remains visible as the redundancy control.

Contact-guided reselection

\[\hat{\mathbf g}_s=\operatorname{orient}_{\rm outward}(\mathbf t_s),\qquad q^*=\arg\max_{q\in\mathcal C}\hat{\mathbf g}_s\!\cdot\!\hat{\mathbf e}_{r,q}.\]

Only after \(N_{\rm bound}=0\), the next eligible contact direction is chosen from the encountered local tangent. This is a declared assumption, not a measured probability law.

Key · released-cell translation

\[\zeta_c\dot{\mathbf r}_c=-\sum_s\mathbf F_s^{\rm cell\to ECM}.\]

The same ECM forces give the cell an equal-and-opposite reaction. A balanced synthetic force pair must not move it.

Drag dissipation

\[W_{\rm diss}(t)=\int_0^t\zeta_b\sum_i|\dot{\mathbf r}_i|^2\,dt.\]

No SLS relaxation is present. Overdamped drag controls the route and timescale toward an elastic configuration.

assumptions and scientific boundary

What can change the conclusion

Explicit assumptions

  • \(R=10\,\mu\mathrm m\), with an implemented but not-yet-run 8–12 µm ensemble sensitivity; circular and constant within each run.
  • 2D uniform random starting angles and stress-free straight fibers.
  • Permanent elastic intersection links with effective \(p_x=0.35\).
  • Outer anchors; convergence must be checked at constant fiber density.
  • Normal traction primary; dipole secondary.
  • Effective Bell clutches; no molecular calibration yet.

Deliberately absent

  • No crosslink breaking or plastic rest-state reset.
  • No SLS material memory or Brownian forcing.
  • No collagen degradation, deposition or biochemical feedback.
  • No cell growth, spheroid biology, deformable cortex or nucleus.
  • No global polarization value.
  • No claim that a 2D link probability transfers to 3D collagen.

Decision rule: alignment is accepted only when a seed-ensemble confidence interval separates from the passive control. One visually convincing trajectory is insufficient. v4-D remains diagnostic until v4-B and v4-C pass their gates.
