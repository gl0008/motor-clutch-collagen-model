# G4 v4E paper ledger

## Riching et al. (2014)

- **Authors:** Kristin M. Riching, Benjamin L. Cox, Max R. Salick, Carolyn Pehlke, Andrew S. Riching, Susan M. Ponik, Benjamin R. Bass, Wendy C. Crone, Yi Jiang, Alissa M. Weaver, Kevin W. Eliceiri, Patricia J. Keely.
- **Title:** 3D Collagen Alignment Limits Protrusions to Enhance Breast Cancer Cell Persistence.
- **Journal/year:** *Biophysical Journal*, 2014.
- **DOI:** [10.1016/j.bpj.2014.10.035](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bpj.2014.10.035)
- **Access:** [Open full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4255204/).
- **System:** MDA-MB-231 cells in 3D collagen; aligned and nonaligned matrices; six-hour trajectories sampled every ten minutes.
- **Supports:** aligned collagen constrains protrusions and increases directional persistence along the alignment axis.
- **Does not support:** the exact exponential selection law, $\beta$ values, 120 s memory, or rear-rebinding rule.

## Carey et al. (2016)

- **Authors:** Shawn P. Carey, Zachary E. Goldblatt, Karen E. Martin, Bethsabe Romero, Rebecca M. Williams, Cynthia A. Reinhart-King.
- **Title:** Local extracellular matrix alignment directs cellular protrusion dynamics and migration through Rac1 and FAK.
- **Journal/year:** *Integrative Biology*, 2016.
- **DOI:** [10.1039/C6IB00030D](https://doi.org/10.1039/C6IB00030D)
- **Access:** [Open full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4980151/).
- **System:** MDA-MB-231 cells and collagen-I matrices; local collagen orientation and protrusion dynamics measured around individual cells.
- **Supports:** cells remodel initially isotropic collagen before directional spreading; short probing protrusions near radially aligned fibers stabilize and enlarge; perpendicular structures are less persistent.
- **Does not support:** a fixed 5 µm reach as a universal parameter, the probability equation, or a calibrated memory time.

## Ray et al. (2017)

- **Authors:** Arja Ray, Oscar Lee, Zaw Win, Rachel M. Edwards, Patrick W. Alford, Deok-Ho Kim, Paolo P. Provenzano.
- **Title:** Anisotropic forces from spatially constrained focal adhesions mediate contact guidance directed cell migration.
- **Journal/year:** *Nature Communications*, 2017.
- **DOI/PMID:** [10.1038/ncomms14923](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms14923); PMID 28401884.
- **Access:** [Open full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5394287/).
- **System:** contact-guidance experiments including MDA-MB-231 cells on aligned patterned substrates.
- **Supports:** anisotropic adhesion geometry can orient trajectories and increase persistence without prescribing a world-axis motor force.
- **Does not support:** direct quantitative transfer of 2D patterned-substrate constants to 3D collagen.

## Model-use rule

The literature establishes the direction of the mechanism, not the exact v4E probability constants. Every unsourced number stays marked provisional. No copyrighted PDF is copied into this repository; the ledger stores DOI and open-access links.
