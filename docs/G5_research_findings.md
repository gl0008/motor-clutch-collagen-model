# G5 Literature Synthesis — Organoid Invasion in Fibrous Collagen

> Deep-research output (2026-09-02), adversarially verified (24/25 claims confirmed, 1 refuted).
> Feeds `docs/G5_organoid_plan.md`. Confidence + vote recorded per finding.

## Physics / properties to model (ranked)

1. **Force-induced radial (TACS-3-like) alignment is a 1st-order causal driver of collective
   invasion** (high, 3-0). Radially aligned fibers → more disseminated clusters than circumferential,
   around the *same* spheroid. Generated by tangential swirling → shear → negative normal stress →
   distally-propagating radial contractile stress.
   *Saraswathibhatla 2025 (bioRxiv 2025.01.31.635980); Su/Kim 2021 (Biomaterials, S0142961221002787);
   Botticelli 2025 (bioRxiv 2025.09.02.673661); Ray 2022 (PMC9033577). Cf. Provenzano TACS-3 / Conklin 2011.*

2. **Nonlinear fiber mechanics (strain-stiffening + buckling + tension-driven alignment) → long-range
   force transmission ~8–10 cell diameters (up to ~200 µm)**, far beyond linear-elastic prediction
   (high, 3-0 / one 2-1). Sets the spatial scale of the remodeling halo.
   *Shenoy/Abhilash/Wang 2014 (arXiv 1409.6216); Tsingos 2023 (Biophys J S0006-3495(23)00325-9);
   Aghvami 2016 (PMC5018119); Mark 2020 (eLife 51912).*
   ⚠ Refuted (1-2): the claim that *discreteness alone* (independent of strain-stiffening) causes
   long-range transmission. Do not attribute it to fibrous discreteness without the nonlinearity.

3. **Matrix plasticity / stress-relaxation is a distinct, tunable 1st-order determinant of
   protease-independent confined migration** (high, 3-0). Cells plastically open 2–3 µm-wide, >50 µm
   channels through matrices with <40 nm pores; high plasticity (~30% at 100 Pa) → ~5× migration vs
   low (~10%).
   *Wisdom 2018 (Nat Commun s41467-018-06641-z).*

4. **Stiffening is directional: LOCAL stiffening promotes collective invasion; GLOBAL/systemic
   stiffening suppresses it** regardless of fiber thickness/pore size (high, 3-0). Model stiffness as
   a heterogeneous field, not one global knob.
   *Ray 2022 (Oncogene, PMC9033577).*

## How others model this

5. **Paradigm 1 — agent/cell core + DISCRETE bead-spring fiber network** (high, 3-0). Coupling schemes:
   Reinhart-King "hand-over-hand" dynamic repetitive traction (Reinhardt 2018, ASME JBME); Tsingos
   2023 hybrid Cellular-Potts + bead-spring, focal-adhesion beads in a 1.25 µm band clamp to lattice;
   Zhang/Schwarz 2024 vertex model of deformable polyhedra + fiber linker springs (arXiv 2403.16784).
   Fiber network = Hookean (intra-fiber) + crosslinker + angular bending springs, overdamped Langevin;
   strain-stiffening E~[N_fibers]^1.09.
   → **Our g2 engine is exactly this paradigm** (bead-spring + crosslinks + bending, overdamped).

6. **Paradigm 2 — continuum/voxel ECM field + agent cells** (high, 3-0). PhysiCell hybrid: ECM voxels
   store fiber orientation/anisotropy/density + contact-guidance rules. Lighter-weight than bead-spring
   at organoid scale, but loses discrete-fiber detail. *Botticelli 2025.*

## Quantitative validation targets

7. **Alignment (time + space):** radial alignment index rises Day1→3, plateaus Day3→5; highest at
   0–50 µm border, persists >100 µm; >0.2 = anisotropic; non-invasive organoids show none.
   *Saraswathibhatla 2025; Ray 2022.*

8. **Displacement scaling:** radial vs tangential exponents **n_r≈1.2 vs n_t≈2.2**; radial exponent
   shifts **α=−2 (linear, ~1 Pa) → −0.2 (>1000 Pa, stiffened)**.
   *Saraswathibhatla 2025; Mark 2020.*

9. **Force / deformation / stiffening:** TNBC spheroid (~4000 cells) → **344±35 µN / 677±68 Pa** over
   24 h; **~200 µm surface deformation (>50% strain)**; **>20× local stiffening** (κ0=1645 Pa).
   *Mark 2020.* (µN is spheroid-aggregate, Pa is pressure not modulus, TNBC not PDAC.)

10. **Best ablation handles:** (a) radial vs circumferential fiber orientation around the *same*
    spheroid (Su/Kim 2021); (b) high vs low plasticity IPN → 5× migration (Wisdom 2018).

## Caveats
- Force/pressure/stiffening magnitudes (344 µN, 677 Pa, >20×) and alignment dynamics are **breast**
  (TNBC/PyMT), treat as cross-tissue anchors, not PDAC-exact.
- Power-law exponents (n_r, n_t; n=0.35–1.09) are **model outputs** from specific 2D geometries —
  reproducing them validates model-vs-model; anchor to measured metrics first.
- Plasticity (Wisdom 2018) and voxel-continuum (Botticelli 2025) each rest on a single primary source
  (both 3-0 verified). Two key refs are preprints (Saraswathibhatla, Botticelli).
- Cell–cell adhesion / jamming and proliferation-pressure were flagged 1st-order but **no surviving
  claim gives a quantitative jamming or proliferation target** — open questions.
