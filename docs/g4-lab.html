The decision that created G4

G3A/B/C 只有 3–8 條、沒有 crosslink、而且每條 fiber 兩端固定；G2 雖然有 99-fiber outer-boundary network，卻把每個 2D intersection 都設為 crosslink。所以下一版最合理的做法不是直接繼續調 G3，而是保留 G3，建立一個新的 elastic calibration generation：沿用 G2 的網路幾何與 boundary anchoring、G3 的 material-point clutch/contact，再把 crosslink 改成有機率的永久 elastic links。先固定 cell 把 ECM 調對，再恢復 protrusion 與 cell movement。

Version-history correction: this diagnosed the superseded early G3 fixtures. The active G3 was later rebuilt with 120–150 fibres, outer-boundary anchoring and a 0.30 crosslink fraction. G4 preserves the reasoning and turns it into a controlled calibration sequence rather than claiming that it is still the current G3 code.

G4A

Tune the basic mechanics with a fixed cell

Change one coefficient at a time around the baseline. This is intentional: if stiffness, drag and link density all change together, a prettier animation cannot tell us which mechanism caused it. G4B

Why can an indirect fibre realign?

Setup, not assumption: Gaussian force is evaluated only after a hard 0–3 µm surface-contact test and is assigned only to the 12 orange direct fibres. An indirect fibre gets zero active force. It can move only when deformation of a permanent material-point crosslink produces Fx=kxδx, transferring force through the graph. The left negative control removes all links on the same geometry.

direct contact fibre
active force→crosslink deformation
kxδx→1-hop fibre
indirect motion→2+-hop fibres
decaying response

Verification: direct, one-hop, two+-hop and unconnected fibres are identified from the actual retained-link graph. Compare their measured displacement and orientation change. If the no-link control moves an indirect class, or linked one-hop fibres remain indistinguishable from unconnected fibres, the proposed transmission mechanism has failed.

G4C

Add pulling → stochastic slip → traction drop → recoil

The ECM and cell constraint from G4B remain. Each surface site now has 12 effective clutches. A bound clutch loads as F=kcx; the Bell off-rate grows continuously as koff=koff0eF/Fb. There is no hard “too long” threshold: larger extension or force makes detachment increasingly likely at every time step.

0 nNx = 0 µm38.5 smedian lifetime at constant force
1.5 nN = Fbx = 0.75 µm14.2 smedian lifetime; hazard ×e
3.0 nN = 2Fbx = 1.50 µm5.2 smedian lifetime; hazard ×e²

Why these defaults? kc=2 nN/µm, kon=0.055 s⁻¹, koff0=0.018 s⁻¹, Fb=1.5 nN, v0=0.025 µm/s and Fstall=8 nN/site are inherited from the repository's G2 V3 effective-clutch mechanism calibration. Bell supports the exponential force sensitivity; Bangasser & Odde support motor–clutch loading; Prahl supports opposing modules. These exact coarse-grained nN values are starting values to sweep against experimental pulling/slip/recoil videos, not direct single-integrin measurements.

What the code solves

KEY · overdamped bead force balance

ζbṙi = Fstretch + Fbend + Fcrosslink + Frepulsion + Factive

Drag dissipates motion; the springs and links store elastic energy. No SLS appears in G4.
probabilistic but permanent links

link exists ⇔ uintersection < px;   Fx=kx[(pb−pa)−ℓx0]

The probability sets initial topology. Retained G4 links do not break.
hybrid direct coupling

0≤dsurface≤3 µm, then wm∝exp(−dm²/σc²), σc=1.5 µm

Gaussian weighting never reaches an indirect fibre.
KEY · stochastic Bell slip

Poff=1−exp[−koff0exp(F/Fb)Δt]

“Too long/heavy” means higher probability, not a deterministic cutoff.

Paper support and evidence boundary

  • Lee et al. (2014) — finite bead–spring collagen fibres and intersection links; does not choose G4's exact link probability.
  • Abhilash et al. (2014) and Wang et al. (2014) — contractile cells reorganize fibrous networks and transmit force nonlocally; do not prove this 2D parameter set.
  • Bell (1978) — exponential force-dependent slip-bond kinetics; does not validate an effective nN clutch as one molecule.
  • Bangasser & Odde (2013) and Prahl et al. (2020) — motor–clutch load-and-fail dynamics and opposing modules; exact G4 values remain calibration assumptions.
