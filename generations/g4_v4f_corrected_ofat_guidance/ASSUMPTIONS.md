# G4 v4F assumption ledger

| ID | Statement | Status | Expected impact | Required check |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| V4F-A001 | A collagen bead inside the exclusion radius exerts an equal-and-opposite force on the cell. | Mechanical consistency; penalty form assumed | Restores two-way steric coupling and removes barrier-free cell passage. | Pair-force residual and symmetric obstacle tests. |
| V4F-A002 | Steric overlap uses the inherited linear penalty stiffness. | Provisional numerical constitutive law | Controls how strongly the collagen cage resists overlap. | Stiffness and timestep sensitivity; no quantitative 3D confinement claim. |
| V4F-A003 | Bound sites retain material identity until complete cluster failure. | Motor–clutch abstraction | Allows loading and recoil without teleporting bound adhesions. | Event log must preserve edge and interpolation coordinate while bound. |
| V4F-A004 | Failed sites search around the current cell every 5 s. | Provisional search clock; shorter than the effective \(1/k_{on}\approx18\,s\) binding time | Enables encounter with new collagen as the cell moves without treating contact availability as continuous. | Check 0.5/2/5 s sensitivity; all accepted sites must lie within current reach. |
| V4F-A005 | E0/E1 rebinding is Gaussian distance-weighted and otherwise directionless. | Null-model definition | Removes the hidden tangent memory present in v4E. | E0/E2 and E1/E3 must match before memory maturation. |
| V4F-A006 | A successful contact lasting 120 s creates front memory only in E2/E3. | Qualitatively supported; duration uncalibrated | Converts a local fiber encounter into temporary directional persistence. | 60/120/180 s sensitivity. |
| V4F-A007 | The one-sided radial tract is generated by rigidly rotating eligible finite fibers. | Experimental construct | Isolates matrix orientation without changing fiber length or force magnitude. | Topology density and connected fraction within 5% of control. |
| V4F-A008 | A single jointly typical seed is used only for animation. | Presentation rule | Prevents an extreme E0 or E3 trajectory from defining the visual conclusion. | Report seed rank and full 20-seed distributions. |
| V4F-A009 | Seed, timestep, event channel and clutch index use independently mixed counter addresses. | Numerical consistency correction | Removes the former seed/site alias while preserving matched streams across E0–E3. | Same address must reproduce exactly; shifting seed and item in opposite directions must not collide; E2 world-axis audit. |

The exact steric stiffness, contact-search interval, front clock, cone width and
guidance gains are assumptions. Riching, Carey and Ray support the qualitative
contact-guidance mechanism; they do not calibrate these model constants.
