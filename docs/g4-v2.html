Why G4 v1 looked static

The mechanism was moving, but the picture could not resolve it.

Time mismatch

G4 v1 showed 2–5 minutes. MDA-MB-231 collagen displacement experiments commonly compare two-hour records with four-minute imaging intervals.

Subpixel indirect motion

One-hop fibres moved orders of magnitude less than directly pulled fibres. At a 180 µm full-field view, much of that response was below one screen pixel.

Hidden individual slips

Five-second website frames skipped many stochastic events, and only total bound count was saved. A rupture could not be assigned to a site or clutch.

Parallel redundancy

Twelve independent clutches let one clutch fail while eleven continue carrying load. That is a slip, but not a visible loss of the whole attachment.

Independent baseline

What every stage actually solves

Orange-bordered equations are the shortest explanation of the mechanism; the remaining equations are still part of the implementation.

Drag dissipates motion; collagen bonds and links store elastic energy. No SLS material memory is present.

The initial curved fibre is stress free; bending penalizes curvature change.

Permanent material-point link

Probability chooses initial topology. G4 v2 links do not break or remodel.

Evidence boundary

References used in G4 v2

  • Riching et al. (2014) — MDA-MB-231 collagen displacement recorded for two hours at four-minute intervals; supports observation scale, not the exact G4 coefficients.
  • Erdmann & Schwarz (2004) — stochastic adhesion clusters under shared linear loading; supports the (i\to i-1) load-sharing rupture equation.
  • Gao et al. (2011) — coupled stochastic–elastic focal contacts and catastrophic failure after load redistribution; supports the comparison hypothesis, not a guaranteed avalanche.
  • Bell (1978) — exponential force-dependent slip-bond hazard.
  • Lee et al. (2014), Abhilash et al. (2014), and Wang et al. (2014) — bead–spring fibrous networks, links, nonlocal force transmission and cell-driven reorganization.

Deliberately absent: SLS, crosslink breaking, plastic rest-state reset, nonlinear strain stiffening, proteolysis, cell deformation, nucleus and 3D pore constraints. G4 v2 remains an elastic single-cell mechanism calibration.
