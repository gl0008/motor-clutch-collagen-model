G3

Contractile spheroid remodels collagen into a radial pattern

G3 is rebuilt directly on Gloria's frozen G2 collagen engine. A cell spheroid starts with a fibre-free gap, sends protrusions out in every direction, grips the collagen with motor–clutches and reels it inward — reorganising the surrounding fibres from a disordered tangle into a radial aster. It removes G2 V3's polarity_probability=0.65: gripping is symmetric and all-around, and the point is the matrix remodelling, not cell motion.

Full field + evidence boundary

What the G3 rebuild shows—and what it does not claim

  • Shows: no contact at t=0 → protrusions reaching out in every direction → motor–clutch reel-in → the collagen reorganising from tangential/random into a radial aster, on the validated G2 engine.
  • Emergent, symmetric: gripping is all-around with no ψ=0.65, no +x force and no prescribed velocity; the cell barely moves.
  • Declared choice: the collagen is softened and its crosslink density reduced relative to the stiff G2 baseline so the near-field fibres can visibly rotate radially.
  • Preserved: G1 and G2 remain frozen; the earlier src/g3/ stage model is archived under legacy/g3_v1_superseded/.
  • Not yet present: nonlinear strain-stiffening, stress relaxation or plasticity, 3D density/pores, deformable cortex/nucleus — the aster here is elastic reorganisation.
