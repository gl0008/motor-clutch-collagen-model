# G4 v4 cumulative decision log

## Literature gate

- **Question:** Is random-to-aligned collagen an established phenomenon, or an
  invented target?
- **Evidence:** Strain experiments, tumor explants, single-cell collagen
  imaging, spheroid studies and fiber-network models independently support
  force-associated alignment.
- **Boundary:** No single paper validates this project’s exact 2D crosslinks,
  Gaussian contact, clutch kinetics and long biological clock together.
- **Decision:** Build cumulatively and label every unsourced coupling.

## v4-A — passive cell and cavity benchmark

- **Passive prediction:** inserting a fixed steric circle without force must not
  change collagen position or order.
- **Cavity prediction:** prescribed contraction can recruit stretching and
  increase near-cavity radial order in a sufficiently connected fiber network.
- **Allowed interpretation:** the cavity validates ECM mechanics only. It cannot
  be used to claim that a tumor cell shrinks.

## v4-B — fixed cell and force direction

- **New factor:** finite-band, Gaussian-weighted traction at continuous collagen
  material points.
- **Primary geometry:** all eligible surface contacts with normal inward force.
- **Control:** two opposite angular sectors with the same total force.
- **Predictions:** transverse force fraction should associate with early
  bending/rotation; axial force fraction should associate with stretch-dominated
  transmission. These are evaluated, not imposed.
- **Gate:** report \(\Delta S_r\), graph-distance displacement, force balance,
  energy ratio and seed uncertainty. Visual drama is not an acceptance test.

## v4-C — motor–clutch cycles

- **New factor:** actin/ECM relative motion loads bound effective clutches;
  Bell rupture interrupts transmission; rebinding restarts it.
- **Key distinction:** continued force after contact comes from clutch extension,
  never from changing cell radius.
- **Gate:** force rises while a clutch remains bound, complete site failure is
  visible only when bound count reaches zero, and the event log, traction drop
  and recoil window share one timestamp.
- **Control retained:** the first short build recorded hundreds of individual
  ruptures but no complete site failure with independent clutches. The shared-
  load cluster is therefore primary and the independent model remains visible
  as the redundancy control; the observation is not hidden by animation.

## v4-D — released cell

- **New factor:** only the cell center is released under the reaction force.
- **No global polarity:** contact positions come from encountered collagen.
- **Gate:** a balanced synthetic reaction gives no translation; fixed and
  released comparisons reuse the same network and random stream.
- **Interpretation limit:** movement is the rigid-body consequence of traction
  imbalance, not a complete model of membrane protrusion or nuclear passage.
