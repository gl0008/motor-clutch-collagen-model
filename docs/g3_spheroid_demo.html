Generation 3 · spheroid collagen remodelling

A contractile cell spheroid starts with no collagen contact, sends protrusions out in every direction, grips the fibres with motor–clutches and reels them inward — reorganising the surrounding collagen from a disordered tangle into a radial pattern. The spheroid itself stays put: the point is the matrix remodelling, not cell motion. Same overdamped bead–spring collagen engine as Gloria's G2, with no prescribed direction and no 0.65 polarity.

What you are watching

The peach spheroid grows green protrusions in every direction. When a tip reaches a fibre it turns orange and a red clutch spoke reels that captured material point inward. All around the cell the fibres are drawn in and rotate to point at the spheroid, so the collagen goes from a disordered tangle into a radial aster — watch the radial-order readout climb from tangential toward radial. Excluded-volume keeps the fibres off the cell body, so it never overlaps them. The cell grips wherever it finds collagen, with no prescribed direction.
