How to read results

What counts as evidence?

Use spatial decay, radial alignment, RMS displacement, stored energy, drag power, clutch imbalance, reaction force, and residual post-unload structure. A visually dramatic movie alone is not validation.

Interpretation boundary: geometry is cell-scale, but force, drag, adhesion and plasticity parameters are not yet calibrated to a chosen cell line and collagen concentration. V4 is a testable mechanism hypothesis, not a claim that its current kinetic law is molecularly correct.
